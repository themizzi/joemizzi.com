The Bollweevils perform Saturday at Dr. Strange's Winter Bash, a two-day, all-ages punk festival. Saturday's lineup includes Adolescents, The Briefs, The Skulls, Michael McColgan and the Bomb Squad, Death by Stereo, Drakulas, The Drowns, Love Canal, Escape from the Zoo, Naked Aggression, Raptors, Pat Todd and the Rankoutsiders, Emily and the Blackouts, Marching Party, Bad Ass, and Sweet Unknown. Doors at 1:30 p.m.; two stages.
