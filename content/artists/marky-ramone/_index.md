Marky Ramone (born Marc Bell) is an American drummer best known for his work with the Ramones. He joined the band in 1978, played with them through 1983, and returned in 1987, remaining until their final show in 1996. He is the Ramones' longest-serving drummer and was inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame with the band in 2002.
