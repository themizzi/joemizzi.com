Blitzkid are a horror-punk band from Bluefield, West Virginia. Formed in 1997 by singer and bassist Argyle Goolsby and singer and guitarist TB Monstrosity, they helped develop and spread the horror-punk sound. The band has released five studio albums, appeared on numerous compilations, and toured extensively in the United States and internationally.

Blitzkid's music combines punk energy with horror-film imagery, dark atmosphere, and melodic songwriting. They reunited for the *Escape the Grave* tour after a period of inactivity and continue to perform.
